# Lauf Seigla tariff exposure

Effective US tariff rates on a Lauf Seigla Club, 20 January 2017 to 10 August 2026, split
between the parts Lauf designs itself and the parts it buys in.

**Live page:** https://chrisjkennedy.github.io/lauf-seigla-tariffs/

## What it shows

| Group | Share of input cost | Rate today | Peak |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whole bike, all 18 components | 100% | 21.71% | 77.45% (10 Apr 2025) |
| Frame, fork, handlebar — China | 39.5% | 39.04% | 171.54% (10 Apr 2025) |
| Everything else — Taiwan and EU | 60.5% | 10.40% | 37.91% (9 Apr 2025) |

The first group is where Lauf's own design sits: the leaf-spring fork the company was
founded on, the glass-fibre-reinforced Smoothie handlebar, and the frame. None can be
re-sourced without new moulds and tooling.

The second group is bought in. Eight of the sixteen parts are SRAM — 33.4% of the bike's
input cost, more than half of this group — and all of it is substitutable catalogue stock.

## Method

Rates come from a daily model of 14 HTS provisions across 5 origins, 20 January 2017 to
10 August 2026. The blended series is the value-share-weighted mean of all 18 components.
Each group series is the weighted mean within that group, so it reads as the rate that
group pays rather than its contribution to the total.

Assumes ordinary consumption entries with no transaction-specific relief; the carbon-frame
Section 301 exclusion is modelled as qualifying; the chain takes the full 50% Section 232
derivative rate from 6 April 2026.
